# Research contribution and limits

This repository is a simulation-oriented comparison of lease-adjusted covenant
metrics and reduced-form analytic screening on synthetic scenarios. Its useful
outputs are explicit cash/debt reconciliations, reproducible model comparisons,
and inspectable assumptions and tests.

The current benchmark does not establish novelty priority, real-company default
prediction, causal effects of IFRS-16, or realized transaction performance. It
does not generate historical success-rate claims, Sobol indices, calibrated
default probabilities, or IRR optimization results. See
[the benchmark protocol](experimental_design.md) for the generated metrics and
[model conventions](model_specification.md) for structural differences.

The separate Accor example illustrates supplied case-study inputs; it does not
turn the synthetic benchmark into audited transaction data. Results remain
sensitive to operating, financing, lease and covenant assumptions. Legacy paper
and archive snapshots require separate review before citation or submission.
